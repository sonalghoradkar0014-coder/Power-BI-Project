# # 🛒 Amazon Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Amazon sales data and provide meaningful insights into sales performance, customer activity, order trends, payment methods, and order status. The dashboard enables users to monitor key business metrics through dynamic visualizations and filters, helping stakeholders make informed business decisions.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance by category, monthly sales trends, payment methods, and order fulfillment status using interactive charts and KPI cards.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Amazon processes thousands of customer orders across multiple product categories every day. Analyzing this large volume of data manually makes it difficult to answer important business questions such as:

- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- How do sales vary throughout the year?
- Which payment methods are preferred by customers?
- What is the overall order fulfillment status?
- How many customers and orders were recorded?
- Which months experience higher or lower sales?

This dashboard addresses these challenges by converting raw sales data into interactive visualizations that support quick and effective business decision-making.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard presents key business metrics along with interactive visualizations that allow users to explore sales performance across different dimensions.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPI cards:

- **Total Sales:** Displays the overall sales generated.
- **Total Customers:** Shows the total number of customers.
- **Total Orders:** Displays the total number of orders received.

These KPIs provide an instant overview of the business performance.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

After analyzing the dashboard, the following insights can be observed:

- Electronics is one of the highest-performing product categories.
- Monthly sales remain relatively stable with slight fluctuations throughout the year.
- Credit Cards contribute the largest share of customer payments.
- Most customer orders are successfully delivered.
- Returned and pending orders account for only a small percentage of total orders.
- The dashboard indicates strong order fulfillment efficiency.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can improve business performance:

- Increase inventory for high-performing product categories.
- Launch promotional campaigns during low-sales months.
- Encourage digital payment methods through cashback and discounts.
- Monitor returned orders to identify quality or logistics issues.
- Improve customer experience by reducing pending deliveries.
- Continue optimizing delivery operations to maintain high delivery success rates.
